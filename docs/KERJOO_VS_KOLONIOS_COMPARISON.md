# Kerjoo vs Kolonios Feature Comparison

**Date:** August 5, 2026  
**Purpose:** Identify feature gaps between Kerjoo (competitor HRIS) and Kolonios

---

## Executive Summary

Kolonios has a solid foundation with attendance, leave, tasks, and RBAC implemented. However, compared to Kerjoo, Kolonios is missing several **core HRIS modules** that are essential for a complete HR management system.

**Key Findings:**
- ✅ **Kolonios Strengths:** Attendance with geo-fencing, RBAC, Tasks/Work Management, Mobile Dashboard
- ❌ **Critical Gaps:** Payroll, Overtime, Claims, Client Visit, Timesheet
- ⚠️ **Partial Implementations:** Leave management (needs enhancement), Settings (needs expansion)

## Gap Analysis Summary

### Critical Gaps (Tier 1 - Must Have)
1. ❌ **Payroll System** - Core HR function
2. ❌ **Overtime Management** - Labor compliance
3. ❌ **Client Visit Management** - Field/sales teams
4. ❌ **Claims/Expense Management** - Reimbursement workflow

### Important Gaps (Tier 2 - Should Have)
5. ❌ **Timesheet Management** - Project time tracking
6. ❌ **Unpaid Leave Management** - Complete leave system
7. ❌ **Reprimand System** - Disciplinary management
8. ❌ **Holiday Calendar** - Company calendar

### Enhancement Gaps (Tier 3 - Nice to Have)
9. ⚠️ **Leave Management Enhancement** - Reports & settings
10. ⚠️ **Approval Rules** - Configurable workflows
11. ⚠️ **Sales Features** - Sales dashboard & products
12. ⚠️ **Advanced Attendance** - Device & profile approvals

---

## Recommendations

### Phase 1: Core HRIS Completion (High Priority)
Focus on these modules for MVP completeness:
1. **Overtime Management** - Essential for labor compliance
2. **Claims/Expense Management** - Important for reimbursement
3. **Client Visit Management** - Critical for field/sales teams

### Phase 2: Financial & Reporting (Medium Priority)
4. **Payroll System** - Complex but core function
5. **Timesheet Management** - Project-based tracking

### Phase 3: Enhancements (Lower Priority)
6. **Reprimand System**
7. **Holiday Calendar**
8. **Advanced Attendance Features**
